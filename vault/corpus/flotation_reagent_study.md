# Flotation Reagent Study: Sulfide Ni-Cu Ore

## Objective
Evaluate the effect of collector dosage on nickel recovery in sulfide Ni-Cu ore flotation.

## Equipment
- Denver D12 flotation cell (2L)
- pH meter Mettler Toledo
- XRF analyzer for grade measurement

## Parameters
| Parameter              | Range          |
|------------------------|----------------|
| Collector (PAX) dosage | 20, 40, 60 g/t |
| pH                     | 9.5            |
| Impeller speed         | 1200 rpm       |
| Conditioning time      | 3 min          |
| Flotation time         | 10 min         |

## Results
| PAX (g/t) | Ni Recovery (%) | Ni Grade (%) |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| 20        | 72.3            | 8.4          |
| 40        | 85.1            | 9.2          |
| 60        | 86.7            | 8.9          |

## Conclusion
40 g/t PAX is the optimal dosage. At 60 g/t, recovery gain is marginal while selectivity decreases.
