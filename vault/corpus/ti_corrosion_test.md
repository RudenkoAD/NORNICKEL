# Ti Alloy Corrosion Resistance Experiment

## Laboratory
Materials Science Lab, Northern R&D Center

## Team
- Lead: A. Petrov
- Analyst: M. Sidorova
- Technician: V. Kozlov

## Material
- **Alloy**: Ti-6Al-4V (standard grade)
- **Surface treatment**: Polished to Ra 0.2 µm

## Test Conditions
| Parameter      | Value          |
|----------------|----------------|
| Medium         | 3.5% NaCl      |
| Temperature    | 25°C, 60°C     |
| Duration       | 720 hours      |
| Method         | Potentiodynamic polarization |

## Results
- Corrosion rate at 25°C: **0.012 mm/year**
- Corrosion rate at 60°C: **0.045 mm/year**
- Pitting potential: **+1.2 V vs SCE**

## Notes
Significant temperature sensitivity observed. Recommend further testing at intermediate temperatures (40°C, 50°C).
