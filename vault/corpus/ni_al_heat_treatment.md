# Ni-Al Alloy Heat Treatment Study (2023)

## Objective
Investigate the effect of heat treatment temperature on the hardness of Ni-Al alloys with 5-8 wt% Al content.

## Experimental Setup
- **Alloy**: Ni-6Al (6 wt% Al, balance Ni)
- **Equipment**: Vacuum furnace VF-1200
- **Heat treatment**: 800°C, 900°C, 1000°C for 4 hours, water quench
- **Measurements**: Vickers hardness (HV), 10 readings per sample

## Results
| Temperature | Hardness (HV) | Std Dev |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| 800°C       | 310           | 12      |
| 900°C       | 410           | 15      |
| 1000°C      | 380           | 10      |

## Conclusion
Optimal hardness achieved at 900°C. At 1000°C, grain growth reduces hardness despite higher solutionizing.
