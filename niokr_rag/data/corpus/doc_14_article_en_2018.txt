---
title: Diethyldithiocarbamate as a selective collector for nickel sulfides
doc_type: article
lang: en
year: 2018
authority: normal
---
Diethyldithiocarbamate (DEDTC) is a thiol collector with higher selectivity for pentlandite over pyrrhotite compared with conventional xanthates. Its stronger affinity for nickel-bearing sulfides can improve nickel selectivity, particularly in ores where pyrrhotite floats readily.

Used as a supplementary collector alongside butyl xanthate, DEDTC can raise nickel recovery and grade without a large increase in total collector dosage. Reported applications use small additions of 5-15 g/t DEDTC on top of the primary xanthate collector.
